# Sources and claims

Research status: **complete**

## Assumptions
- The Moon disappears instantly and completely at t = 0: its mass, gravity and reflected light all vanish. No debris, no impact.
- This is physically impossible (mass-energy cannot simply vanish); it is a thought experiment used to isolate what the Moon does for Earth.
- Everything else in the Solar System continues unchanged.
- Long-term results for Earth's tilt come from numerical models of a moonless Earth; they are not observations.

## Open questions
- How chaotic would a moonless Earth's tilt really be? Laskar (1993) vs Lissauer et al. (2011) differ; Li & Batygin (2014) add nuance.
- How much would loss of moonlight cues reduce coral spawning success?
- The exact modern rate of day lengthening (1.7 vs ~2.3–2.5 ms per century) depends on method and time window.
